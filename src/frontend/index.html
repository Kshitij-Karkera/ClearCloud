Connect an account

A read-only role in your AWS account that ClearCloud can assume. Leave empty to scan the account hosting ClearCloud, if the site owner allows it.

How to connect your own AWS account
  1. In your AWS account, open IAM, choose Roles and then Create role. Pick Custom trust policy and paste the trust policy below. Keep the placeholder for now.
  2. Attach the permissions policy below to the role, and name the role ClearCloudReadOnly.
  3. Copy the role's ARN into the Role ARN box above and press Connect account.
  4. Copy the External ID shown on the new account card. Then edit the role's trust policy and replace PASTE-EXTERNAL-ID-HERE with it.
  5. Press Scan now.

The ClearCloud role ARN is not published on this site yet. Ask the site owner for it and put it in the trust policy.
